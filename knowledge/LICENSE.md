## Public-sector text and Chinese adaptations

Contains public sector information licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

The Chinese `document.zh-CN.md` files are condensed adaptations, not approved translations or complete reproductions. They do not inherit the original publisher's clinical approval and do not imply endorsement. Source identifiers and URLs in `provenance.json` identify the extraction history, not approval of the adaptation.

## MedlinePlus health-topic summaries

Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine.

The `source.nlm.en.md` files reproduce only the health-topic summary text identified in the corresponding `provenance.json`. MedlinePlus lists health-topic summaries as public-domain content under U.S. law and permits reproduction and redistribution; see [Linking to and Using Content](https://medlineplus.gov/about/using/usingcontent/). This does not apply to all content on that website. No A.D.A.M. encyclopedia, ASHP drug monographs, images or linked third-party material are included.

## Publication is not clinical release

These files are shared for reproducible import, chunking, indexing and evaluation. `PENDING_REVIEW`, `NOT_REVIEWED`, scope warnings and historical permission blockers in the bound provenance are intentionally retained. This notice records the public text redistribution basis checked on 2026-10-11; it does not mutate the original intake evidence or certify medical, translation, scope or patient-facing-use review.

No runtime database approval, hospital affiliation, diagnosis, treatment recommendation or clinical fitness is conferred by committing these files. Import still creates a pending item; approval and indexing remain separate operations.
